## Thirteen data gaps, in plain words, with owners
| # | Gap | In plain words | Effect today | Owner → reviewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No customer key | No single field says "this is the same person" across the systems that hold customer records (CRM AUH, CRM Assets, My Leads, Loyalty portal, Avaya, Convolo…). Ahmed enquires on Bayut, rents through Assets as "Ahmad", asks about a mortgage in My Leads: four records, one man. | His complaints never add up to a pattern; his silence shows nowhere; cases go to whoever owns a fragment | IT (CRM owner) → Internal Audit |
| 2 | No event time for things outside the CRM | The CRM activity does carry an SLA and its own system time, so the CRM side is covered. But a call is answered in Avaya and a WhatsApp reply happens on the phone; their time reaches the CRM only when a person creates the activity later, so the activity time is the logging time, not the event time. Interaction type and attendance are typed by hand; the daily report closes at 19:00. | Reply time and promise-kept measured from when someone logged the event, not when it happened; leads after 19:00 counted next day | IT → Ahmad Kudair + AI engineers (Avaya/chat/AI logs), Sales Support |
| 3 | No lead status / lost-reason list | No agreed list of what a lead's status can be or why it was lost, so everyone classifies their own way. | 3,612 of 5,445 chats "Not a lead" with no reason; conversion rate inflated three times | Sales Support → Marketing |
| 4 | No complaint taxonomy + SLA | No list of complaint types, no owner or deadline per type, no reference number for the customer. | Escalation matrix cannot route a free-text complaint; no clock; "3 past SLA" cannot be reported | Operations → Internal Audit |
| 5 | No interaction-type list / IVR codes | Calls and chats are not tagged by what they are, so customer and non-customer calls are mixed in every count. | Abandonment and call-to-lead far worse than reality | Ahmad Kudair + AI engineers → Sales Support |
| 6 | No consent / DNCR fields | No field records whether a customer agreed to be recorded, surveyed or messaged, or asked never to be called; the AI caller runs with no such rule. | 139,228 AI calls on one campaign with no rule; survey-by-call results include people who should never have been called | Legal/Compliance → Ahmad Kudair + AI engineers; Marketing for campaigns |
| 7 | No promise record | A promise to a customer ("I'll send the plan today") is not a record with a due time, so nobody can see which promises were kept. | Only the 4 calls/week the trainer hears are checked; "promise not kept" uncountable | Sales Support → IT |
| 8 | No customer owner / hand-off | Nobody is named owner of a customer with more than one unit or product; hand-offs between teams are not recorded. | Faisal (Dubai unit + Abu Dhabi rental) calls; three teams see him, none owns him; case goes by rotation to a stranger | Sales Support → Operations |
| 9 | No agent offboarding | When an agent leaves, nothing removes access or moves their leads. | 251 agents all "Active"; leads on people who left in March; patterns point at ghosts | HR Operations → Sales Support |
| 10 | Survey scales and triggers | Teams ask different questions on different scales at different moments (1–5, % satisfied, 0–10); nothing stops a double survey or a survey to an open-complaint customer. | CX indicators cannot be put on one line | Marketing → Internal Audit [Proposed: confirm Marketing accepts surveys] |
| 11 | QA score not joined to the call | The call's quality score is on a Draft sheet without the call id, so the portal cannot find which call was scored. | Weekly sample shows a call as fine while the trainer scored it 52/100 | Ahmad Kudair + AI engineers → L&D |
| 12 | No version/date/approver | Most documents have no version, date or approver, so nobody can say which text is the approved one (Loyalty SOP is the only exception). | A rule shown as approved with no approval behind it; a disputed score has no text to point to | Internal Audit → each document's writer |
| 13 | No developer/vendor reply record | When PSI escalates to a developer or vendor, their reply and its date are not recorded, so the customer's wait is invisible. | Customer waits 20 days on a snag; portal sees no breach | Operations (After-Sales, Assets) → BD for Loyalty vendors |
