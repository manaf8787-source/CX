# Decisions of 19 November 2026 (Manaf Obeidat, CX manager)

1. **Escalation matrix cells set for all six products** (Decision 4 closed). File: ESCALATION-MATRIX-2026-11-19.md. Two new rows: "Data exposure or system access incident" and "Any kind, VIP customer involved". Three rules: every customer is treated as a VIP and a delay is a failure (respond 1–4 working hours; a CRM VIP tag halves both clocks, informs the head of the environment at once, CX manager backup); information security sits beside Legal on anything that touches data (Wallet and Club product rows inform Information Security by default; conversation text and names never leave the CX team); the receiver is the one who can fix it, never top management, the CX team keeps the customer side, and a fix that does not recover the customer is a failed fix. Product owners may adjust a title with the Internal Audit and Marketing Lead before 28 November; the clocks stay.
2. **The Internal Audit and Marketing Lead audits, never approves fixes.** Approval belongs to the responsible manager by title from the matrix, from their inbox.
3. **The weekly sample is a spot-check of the AI's work, one piece at a time**, with what the AI was given, what it decided, and one question (would you have done the same?). Split by reader: CX team reads fixes, briefs and readings; Internal Audit reads classification, routing, access and targeting, ids and titles only. A "no" lowers the agent's pass rate and becomes a supervisor test.
4. **Campaign audit.** Every campaign, Marketing's and the CX team's AI interview campaigns, is cleared or sent back by the Internal Audit and Marketing Lead before anything reaches a customer (right people, right projects and developers, right channel and message, the money) and audited after the run (share matching the persona, below 80% raises a targeting signal; response; results; cost per result).
5. **"Watch the AI team work"** page: the mechanism shown as a replay; impact figures only after Shadow on real data.
