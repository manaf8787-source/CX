# Escalation matrix cells (set 19 Nov 2026)

Titles only; the CRM supplies who holds the title. Clocks in working hours (Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00). Set by the CX manager on 19 Nov; product owners may adjust a title with the Internal Audit and Marketing Lead before 28 Nov; the clocks stay. The rows "Data exposure" and "VIP" are the same for every product.

## Three rules

1. Every customer is treated as a VIP; a delay is a failure. Respond clocks are 1 to 4 working hours for anything a customer can feel. A person tagged VIP in the CRM halves both clocks, informs the head of the environment at once, and makes the CX manager the backup. The resolve clock is the plan and the first visible step, not the end of the work.
2. Information security sits beside Legal on anything that touches data; a data-exposure incident is its own row; Wallet and Club product rows inform Information Security by default; conversation text and names never leave the CX team in an escalation.
3. The receiver is the one who can fix it, never top management; heads are informed; an employee kind goes to the direct manager only; the CX team keeps the customer side while the fix is made; a fix that does not recover the customer is a failed fix.
